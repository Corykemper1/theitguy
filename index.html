Common questions

Before you send the ticket.

Have a scope, checklist, or remote contact already? Include it with your request.

Can you work with our remote IT team or MSP?

Yes. We can follow an established scope, coordinate during the visit, provide visual confirmation, and document the physical work for your team.

What kinds of sites do you support?

We focus on commercial environments, offices, multi-site businesses, and facilities that need on-site IT or low-voltage assistance.

What should I include in a service request?

Include the site address, best on-site contact, desired timing, symptoms or scope, access requirements, and any remote support contact or ticket number.

Do you take after-hours requests?

Service requests are accepted 24/7. Include your preferred timing and urgency so we can confirm the appropriate next step.

Can you maintain documentation for multiple locations?

Yes. Ask about ongoing site records for equipment, ports, model and serial information, photos, and service history.
