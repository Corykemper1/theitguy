Privacy notice

How service-request information is handled.

Last updated July 28, 2026

Information we collect

When you submit a service request, we may collect your name, company, email address, phone number, site location, requested service, and the information you include in your message.

How we use it

We use this information to review your request, contact you, coordinate service, maintain business records, and improve our service process. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers

Our website uses Formspree to process service-request forms. Information submitted through the form may be stored or processed by Formspree and delivered to our business email provider. Those providers handle information under their own privacy and security terms.

Retention and security

We retain service-request information as reasonably needed for communication, service records, business operations, and legal obligations. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may request that we correct or delete contact information that you submitted, subject to records we must retain for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Contact

For a privacy question or request, call or text (785) 230-8156 or use the service-request form.
